About

I'm Dan Biagini, founder of Top Shelf Technology LLC. I've spent twenty-five years building software and leading engineering teams, and I've been through three acquisitions (one leading technical due diligence from the sell side).

Today my practice is a small fractional CTO and engineering consulting business. At Laconic, where I was VP of Engineering, I'm now a consulting engineer delivering the next generation of SADAR — a platform that measures and visualizes carbon emissions and removals using geospatial data, ML pipelines and agentic AI. I'm also an engineering consultant at LexData Labs, putting a real release process in place and mentoring their engineers, and I have run architecture reviews and system audits for early-stage startups.

Before Laconic, I ran engineering at Yottaa, a high-growth B2B SaaS, where I grew the team from 9 to 30 engineers and led them through a PE acquisition in 2022. Before that, I led an engineering team of 50 at Ericsson, and built software at Akamai, Azuki (acquired by Ericsson and Microsoft), and Cisco.

I was also co-founder and CTO of Realia, where I built and launched an AI-native investment platform from scratch. Today I'm co-founder of Tyldan Labs, where we're building Signals. I've sat on both sides of the table — advising founders on their tech strategy and pitching investors on my own.

I take on one or two additional fractional CTO clients at a time.
